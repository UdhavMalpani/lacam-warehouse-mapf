Inside LaCAM · why PIBT needs a search

PIBT alone livelocks just short of the goal

PIBT gets 95–99.5 % home, then cycles: 95 of 100, 284 of 300, 490 of 500 and 199 of 200 at best, over 10,000 steps.

LaCAM sees the cycle as a repeated configuration and searches around it.

Swap or search? In aisles the swap rule alone fixes it; on the random map only the search does.

These traces show the share of agents standing on their goal at each timestep, on four instances where plain PIBT fails. PIBT gets almost everyone home quickly, then a handful of agents cycle forever: at best 95 of 100, 284 of 300, 490 of 500 and 199 of 200 agents, over 10,000 timesteps. This is a livelock: agents with low priority keep being pushed off their goals by others. LaCAM* finishes each in under 300 timesteps. Note the different fixes: in the three aisle layouts PIBT with the swap technique already succeeds, because the deadlock is two agents meeting head-on in a corridor. On random-32-32-20 even PIBT with swap stays stuck at 197 of 200, and only LaCAM's search over configurations gets out, which the original LaCAM, without swap, does in 98 timesteps.
