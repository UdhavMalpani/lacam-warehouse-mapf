This animation plays the same 100-agent instance on the dead-end aisle layout. On the left, plain PIBT: by around timestep 170 about 94 agents are parked, and the remaining ones keep shuffling at aisle entrances without ever finishing. On the right, LaCAM*: everyone is home at timestep 177, which equals the makespan lower bound (the longest single shortest path) for this instance.
