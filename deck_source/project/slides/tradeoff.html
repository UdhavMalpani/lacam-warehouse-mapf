Results · speed versus quality

Fast and costlier, or slow and near-optimal

Medians over each planner's own solved instances: a planner that solves more also faces a harder mix, which pushes its quality number up.

Each planner as one point: median runtime against median sum-of-costs over lower bound. Two clusters appear. The PIBT family (PIBT, LaCAM, LaCAM*) is at tens of milliseconds with costs 25 to 48% above the lower bound. The search and LNS baselines take seconds and are near-optimal (EECBS and PBS within about 1% of the lower bound on the instances they solve). Caveat: this summary is over each planner's own solved set, and LaCAM* solved the most and hardest instances, which inflates its median cost. The previous slide's paired comparison is the fair version. For warehouses, where replanning must be fast and robust, the left cluster is attractive; closing its quality gap by refinement is the obvious next step (see the anytime slide).
