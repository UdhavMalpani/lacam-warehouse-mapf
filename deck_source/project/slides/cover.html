LaCAM in the warehouse

Re-implementing LaCAM, a search built on PIBT, and testing it against PIBT, EECBS, MAPF-LNS2 and PBS on 7 warehouse layouts and 5 other domains

Udhav Malpani · [course] · [instructor]

7 planners · 12 maps · 4,500 runs

This talk has two parts. First I re-implemented LaCAM, the search-based MAPF planner that uses PIBT as its engine, and checked it against the official code. Then I benchmarked it against plain PIBT and three state-of-the-art planners from other algorithm families: EECBS (bounded-suboptimal conflict-based search), MAPF-LNS2 (large-neighbourhood search) and PBS (priority-based search), on seven warehouse layouts plus five non-warehouse maps.
