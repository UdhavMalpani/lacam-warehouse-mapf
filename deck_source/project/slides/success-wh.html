Results · success rate, warehouse maps

On warehouse floors LaCAM* solves every instance

Success rate within 30 seconds versus number of agents, one panel per warehouse map, 10 instances per point. LaCAM* (blue) is at 100% everywhere, up to 1,000 agents. The interesting failures: on the 1-wide-aisle maps (top-left, third panel, sortation) and especially the dead-end narrow-aisle layout, plain PIBT and the original LaCAM collapse early, because agents meeting head-on in a 1-wide aisle cannot pass. PBS fails first among the baselines, then EECBS, then MAPF-LNS2. On the open cross-dock and the 2-wide-aisle warehouses almost everything works, except PBS beyond 600 to 800 agents. At 1,000 agents on warehouse maps the success rates are LaCAM* 100%, PIBT with swap 84%, MAPF-LNS2 47%, LaCAM 43%, PIBT 43%, EECBS 41%, PBS 0%.
