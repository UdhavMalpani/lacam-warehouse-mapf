Appendix · quality, other domains

Sum-of-costs over lower bound by agent count

Outside the warehouse, quality depends strongly on how constrained the map is. On the large open game and city maps LaCAM* stays below 1.4 even at 1,000 agents. In the room map (narrow doors) it reaches 5.7 at 1,000 agents and in the maze 7.6 at 350 agents: long queues through bottlenecks, which a first-solution planner does nothing to shorten. These are the cases where post-optimisation such as LNS refinement would pay off most.
