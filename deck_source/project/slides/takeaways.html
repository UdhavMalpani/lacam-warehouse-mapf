Takeaways

What this study shows about LaCAM

01 · mechanism

LaCAM makes PIBT complete almost for free: it searches only when PIBT starts cycling.

02 · warehouses

The swap technique is essential: 1-wide and dead-end aisles break plain PIBT and the original LaCAM.

03 · versus the state of the art

LaCAM* solved 760 of 760 instances, median 45 ms on warehouses, 35–84× faster than EECBS, MAPF-LNS2 and PBS.

04 · the price

Plans cost 14–31 % more, and LaCAM*'s own refinement barely helps; polish its first plan with LNS instead.

Four things to remember. One: LaCAM is a thin layer over PIBT. When PIBT works, LaCAM's search costs nothing extra; when PIBT starts cycling, LaCAM detects the repeated configuration and searches around it, which makes the planner complete. Two: in warehouses the swap technique matters as much as the search, because head-on encounters in 1-wide aisles and dead ends are the dominant failure. Three: LaCAM* was the only planner to solve all 760 instances, with a median of 45 ms on warehouse maps, 35 to 84 times faster than the state-of-the-art baselines on the instances they also solved. Four: the trade-off is quality, 14 to 31% higher sum-of-costs, and LaCAM*'s built-in refinement improves only about 2% within a minute, so for quality one should hand its first plan to an LNS refiner.
