Median time to solution by agent count

Median time to solution, where failures count as time-outs, so a point is drawn only where at least half the instances were solved. LaCAM* (blue) grows from a few milliseconds at 100 agents to 0.1 to 0.65 seconds at 1,000 agents. The original LaCAM and PIBT overlap with LaCAM* wherever they succeed. The baselines are two orders of magnitude slower and approach the 30 second limit, drawn as the top line, as density grows.
