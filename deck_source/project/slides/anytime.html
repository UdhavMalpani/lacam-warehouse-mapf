Inside LaCAM · anytime refinement, 60 s

Refinement: LaCAM* stalls, MAPF-LNS keeps going

LaCAM*: −1.7 % median in 60 s. It stores every configuration, so 33 of 45 runs hit the 1.2 GB cap.

MAPF-LNS: −11.4 % median, up to −21 %.

But LaCAM* answers 37× sooner: 14 ms versus 0.5 s to the first solution.

Here both planners were allowed 60 seconds to keep improving: LaCAM* with its built-in refinement (re-wiring plus pruning, eventually optimal) and MAPF-LNS, which starts from the LNS2 solution and repeatedly re-plans small groups of agents. Each line goes from the first solution (hollow marker) to the cost after 60 seconds (filled marker), median of 3 instances. LaCAM*'s refinement improved the cost by only 1.7% at the median (8% at best). It keeps every explored configuration in memory, and 33 of 45 runs reached the 1.2 GB per-run cap after a median of 19 seconds. MAPF-LNS improved by 11.4% at the median and up to 21%, ending about 1.33 times cheaper than LaCAM*. But LaCAM* produced its first solution in 14 ms versus 0.5 s. A natural next step is to polish LaCAM*'s first plan with an LNS-style refiner. The follow-up paper "Engineering LaCAM*" (Okumura, AAMAS 2024) addresses this solution-quality gap with further improvement techniques; I did not evaluate it.
