The price of speed: 14–31 % higher cost

Paired comparison on instances both planners solved, so no planner is judged on an easier subset. LaCAM and PIBT give LaCAM*'s plans whenever they succeed.

To compare quality fairly I only use instances that both planners solved, and take the ratio of LaCAM*'s sum-of-costs to the other planner's on the same instance. Against EECBS (suboptimality bound 1.2, so within 20% of optimal) LaCAM* is 31% more expensive at the median; against PBS 26%; against MAPF-LNS2 14%. The ratio to LaCAM and PIBT is exactly 1.00 at the median: when they succeed they produce essentially the same plan, because all three use the same PIBT steps and LaCAM's search only kicks in when PIBT gets stuck. The absolute numbers (sum-of-costs over the lower bound, by map and agent count) are in the appendix: LaCAM* grows from about 1.1 to 1.3 at 100 agents to between 1.4 and 3.7 at 1,000 agents on the warehouse maps, and up to 5.7 in the room map and 7.6 in the maze at their largest sizes, where agents must queue through narrow doors and corridors.
