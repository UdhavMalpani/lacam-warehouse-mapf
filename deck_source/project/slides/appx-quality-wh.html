Sum-of-costs over lower bound by agent count

Absolute quality: sum-of-costs divided by the sum of shortest-path distances (a lower bound), median over solved instances, shown only where at least half were solved. EECBS and PBS stay within a few percent of the bound where they finish. MAPF-LNS2 sits in between. LaCAM* starts around 1.06 to 1.30 at 100 agents and rises to 1.38 to 3.73 at 1,000 agents, highest on the 1-wide and dead-end layouts, where agents must wait for each other in narrow aisles.
